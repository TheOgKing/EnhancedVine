#  📱 EnhancedVine: Installation & Feature Guide
Since EnhancedVine is distributed as a pre-compiled .ipa file, you will need to "sideload" the application onto your iOS device. Below is a comprehensive guide on how to get started and a deep dive into the features that will transform your Amazon Vine experience.

#### 📥 How to Install (Sideloading Tutorial)
Because this app is not on the App Store, you must use a sideloading tool to install the .ipa on your iPhone or iPad.

- Option 1: SideStore (Recommended - No Computer Needed After Setup)
- Download the IPA: Download the latest release from the [Releases] section of this repository.

- Initial Setup: Follow the SideStore Setup Guide (requires a computer once to install the SideStore app).

- Install: Open SideStore on your phone, tap the + icon, and select the EnhancedVine.ipa file.

- Refresh: Remember to refresh the app every 7 days via SideStore to keep it active.

#### Option 2: AltStore
- Install AltServer on your PC or Mac.

- Connect your iPhone to your computer via lightning/USB-C.

- Open the AltStore app on your phone, go to My Apps, tap +, and select the downloaded EnhancedVine.ipa.

#### Option 3: Sideloadly
- Download and install Sideloadly.

- Connect your device to your computer.

- Drag the EnhancedVine.ipa into the Sideloadly window.

- Enter your Apple ID and click Start.

# 🌟 Extensive Feature Guide
EnhancedVine is a specialized toolset designed to give power users an edge in the Amazon Vine ecosystem.

### ⚡ Automation & Workflow (Script Engine)
The heart of the app is a modular engine that injects custom logic directly into the Vine interface:

- Infinite Scroll: Never click "Next" again. Items load automatically as you scroll.

- EZ Order: Optimizes the request flow to help you secure high-competition items instantly.

- Auto-Spinner Fix: Detects and resolves the "infinite loading" bug that often breaks the request modal.

- Review Countdown: Helps you track exactly when your reviews are due to maintain your tier status.

### 🎨 Custom Interface (Theming)
The app uses "App Style Modules" to completely overhaul how Amazon Vine looks:

- Modern & Classic Modes: Choose between a cutting-edge UI or a more traditional layout.

- Clutter Reduction: The app injects "Critical CSS" that hides intrusive Amazon headers and footers, maximizing the number of products you see on screen.

- Animated Navigation: Features a custom-built bottom tab bar with fluid, spring-animated transitions.

### 🔍 Power Search Tools
- Saved Search Bar: A persistent, scrollable bar at the top of the browser for your most frequent queries.

- Quick-Save: When you search for something new, a "💾" icon appears to let you save that term instantly for future use.
